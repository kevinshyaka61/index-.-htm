Rayon Sports interim president Murenzi Abdallah has challenged the team to win the 2026/27 BK Pro League title after their elimination from the CAF Confederation Cup. The Rwandan side were knocked out by Mozambique’s Black Bulls in the second qualifying round, losing 4-2 on aggregate over two legs. Speaking to Rayon Sports supporters who welcomed the team in Maputo on Saturday, October 24, Murenzi said the club would now focus on the domestic league, where winning the title remains a key target agreed upon by the management, coaching staff and players. “It is difficult to compete on two fronts. Our players were very focused on these matches because they were important. Our commitment to the coach and his technical team, as well as to the players, is that we must win this season’s league title,” he said. Rayon Sports return to the BK Pro League with ground to make up after several fixtures were postponed due to their participation in continental competition. After six matchdays in the league, the club have played only twice, drawing against Sunrise FC and losing to Gorilla FC. They have one point and sit at the bottom of the standings. Rayon Sports are expected to host Mukura VS in their next league fixture before travelling to face Marine FC. They will then visit Sudanese side Al Hilal SC. The club, which plays in blue and white, last won the Rwandan league title in the 2018/19 season.

Bayern Munich to take on Ajax in Kenya stadium opener

German football giants Bayern Munich will face Dutch record champions Ajax in Nairobi on January 5, 2027, in a match marking the official opening of Kenya’s new national stadium ahead of the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON). The match will take place at the Raila Odinga International Stadium at Talanta Sports City, a 60,000-seat facility built as part of Kenya’s preparations to co-host AFCON 2027 with Rwanda’s neighbours Uganda and Tanzania. The fixture will bring two of Europe’s prominent clubs to East Africa as the region prepares to host the continental tournament, which will be staged across three countries for the first time. The 2027 edition will also mark AFCON’s return to East Africa after more than 50 years. FC Bayern board member for marketing and sales Rouven Kasper said the match would help the German club strengthen its relationship with supporters across Africa. “FC Bayern has millions of fantastic fans in Africa with whom we feel a close connection. It’s a continent with a huge passion for football,” Kasper said. He added that the event would give African supporters an opportunity to experience the club first-hand while raising the profile of German football and the Bundesliga.


The match will take place at the Raila Odinga International Stadium at Talanta Sports City, a 60,000-seat facility built as part of Kenya’s preparations to co-host AFCON 2027.

Ticket sales are scheduled to begin on November 5 through the stadium’s official website. Kenyan President William Ruto announced the fixture on October 28, describing it as a meeting between two major European clubs. He noted that Bayern Munich and Ajax have won Europe’s top club competition a combined 10 times. The stadium, located at Jamhuri Grounds along Ngong Road in Nairobi, is one of Kenya’s key infrastructure projects for AFCON 2027. Construction began in March 2024, with the government overseeing the works through the Ministry of Defence and the Kenya Defence Forces. The facility is designed primarily for football and rugby, with spectators seated closer to the playing surface than in traditional multipurpose stadiums. Beyond AFCON 2027, the stadium is expected to expand Kenya’s capacity to host international sporting events.
